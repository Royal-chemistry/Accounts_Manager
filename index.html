Business & month

How it works

  1. Add your staff once: name, team or branch, monthly salary and bank/UPI details. Or import a CSV.
  2. Each month, enter days worked, extra shifts, advances, food and other cuts.
  3. Get the salary sheet: net pay for everyone, team totals, printable payslips and a payout list for your bank.
  4. Keep a cash book of income and expenses. Salaries are added in automatically, so you see your real profit for the month.

Works for shops, restaurants, clinics, construction crews, cleaning and security agencies — anyone paying staff by days or shifts. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; use Backup to move your data to another device.
