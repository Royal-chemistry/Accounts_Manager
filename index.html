Agency & month

How it works

  1. Add your guards once: name, site, monthly salary and bank/UPI details. Or import a CSV.
  2. Each month, fill the duty register: duties, special duties, advances, mess days, uniform cuts.
  3. Get the salary sheet: net pay for every guard, site totals, printable payslips and a payout list for your bank.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use Backup to save a file you can restore on another device.
